    Workflow

    Benchmarks
    
          
    Interactive timing ≠ controlled repeats ≠ Google Benchmark microbenchmarks. Labels stay honest.

    Saved experiments, benchmark history, shareable runs, and accounts only when real persistence exists. No fake login.
